**First, run step 0 of How round skills work in the root `CLAUDE.md`: get to the DISCOTECH folder.** Then follow the rest of those steps.

## Ask

If the person hasn't already said, ask exactly this and stop until they answer:

> **Who are we helping, and with what?**
> Type what the room picked, or your own idea. For example: a best man dreading the wedding speech.

## Read

Nothing. This round starts from their answer.

## Write

**`company/business.md`** (under 30 lines): `# The business`, then the problem, who has it, why now, how it could make money, and what we're not doing.

**`customers/personas/customer.md`** (under 30 lines): `# Our customer`, then a first name and one-line description, their situation, what keeps them up at 2am, what they've tried, words they use, what would make them say yes, and where to find them.

Make the customer specific and human. Mark guesses `_(assumed)_`.

## Finish

**Your question:** "Does this customer sound like a real person? Tell me what's off and I'll fix it, or say 'looks good'."
